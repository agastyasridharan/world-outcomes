# World outcomes

Viewer for a study of whether post-trained language models steer stories toward endings that are good for the world.

30 short stories stop at a turning point. Ten models (OLMo 3 at six training stages, Gemma 3 base and instruct, Qwen 2.5 base and instruct) each continued every story 32 times as plain text. A judge recorded how each story ended.

Open the site to see the results, read every story and continuation, and check each judge label.

69 of the 9,600 continuations are not included: 63 tripped a content filter and the judge refused 6.
